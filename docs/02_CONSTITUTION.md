# ModeAlert Constitution

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent principles of ModeAlert.

Architectural decisions may change.

Technologies may change.

Business models may evolve.

These principles should not.

---

# Article I

ModeAlert is a business.

Not a coding exercise.

Every decision should consider long-term sustainability.

---

# Article II

Maintainability is more important than speed.

Shortcuts become technical debt.

Technical debt becomes maintenance cost.

Maintenance cost destroys passive income.

---

# Article III

Everything should be modular.

Games.

Providers.

Notifications.

Storage.

Services.

Nothing should depend on a single implementation.

---

# Article IV

Business logic belongs in services.

Never inside API routes.

Never inside UI components.

Never inside providers.

---

# Article V

Every provider should be replaceable.

Removing Riot should not affect CommunityDragon.

Adding Steam should not affect Riot.

Providers are independent.

---

# Article VI

Everything should be testable.

If something cannot be tested,

its design should be questioned.

---

# Article VII

Automation is preferred over manual work.

The computer should do repetitive work.

Not the developer.

---

# Article VIII

The project should remain understandable.

Future Deniz should understand today's code.

Future contributors should understand the project within hours.

---

# Article IX

Avoid overengineering.

Build for future growth.

Not imaginary complexity.

---

# Article X

Every important architectural decision must be documented.

Code without explanation becomes legacy code.

---

# Article XI

The project should survive technology changes.

Frameworks come and go.

Architecture lasts.

---

# Article XII

Revenue should not increase operational complexity.

Every new revenue source should scale without requiring proportional human effort.

---

# Article XIII

Prefer boring technology.

Stable software beats fashionable software.

---

# Article XIV

If documentation and implementation disagree,

implementation must be updated

or documentation must be updated immediately.

---

# Final Principle

Always optimize for the company that ModeAlert will become,

not the project it is today.
